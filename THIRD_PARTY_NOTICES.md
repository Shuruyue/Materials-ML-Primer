## PyXtal: Wyckoff-set table

`materials_ml_primer/wyckoff_sets.json` is derived from `pyxtal/database/wyckoff_sets.json` of PyXtal 1.1.5 (<https://github.com/MaterSim/PyXtal>) by `_build/make_wyckoff_sets.py`, which keeps only the permutations of the Wyckoff letters. PyXtal's table lists, for each space group, the coset representatives of its normalizer and how they map the Wyckoff positions onto each other, as tabulated by the Bilbao Crystallographic Server (program WYCKSETS; M. I. Aroyo et al., *Z. Kristallogr.* **221**, 15 (2006)).
